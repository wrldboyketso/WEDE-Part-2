Our Story

Maka Nana Shisanyama is a small food business in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. A grandmother founded the business in 2015. It started with cooking for the local community, and has grown into a well loved shisanyama near Spar. We now have thousands of followers on TikTok, where our customers share their food experiences.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring warmth and culture to people through food, and to make it easy for anyone to enjoy a meal, no matter where they are from.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a well known and trusted shisanyama brand in Durban, known for good food and a simple way to order.
